Google SRE monitoring: page on symptoms, diagnose with causes

Useful monitoring separates what users experience from why it happens, combines black-box symptoms with white-box diagnosis, and treats every human page as an expensive actionable decision interface.

MENTAL MODEL

BLACK BOX

What users see

Externally visible behavior tells you that a real symptom is happening now and disciplines paging toward user impact.

WHITE BOX

Why it happens

Internal metrics, logs and instrumentation expose causes, imminent failures and problems hidden by retries.

Source problem

A monitoring stack can collect large volumes of internal telemetry yet still fail operators when it cannot separate user-visible symptoms from causes or when noisy alerts interrupt humans without a clear action.

Source thesis

Design monitoring around symptom/cause questions, combine black-box and white-box perspectives, cover core service behavior with a small signal set, and reserve pages for urgent actionable conditions with high signal and low noise.

WHERE THE MODEL BREAKS

Useful does not mean sufficient.

  1. Black-box monitoring is strong for active symptoms but may miss imminent failures before users are affected.
  2. White-box monitoring can explain causes but becomes noisy when internal anomalies are promoted directly to pages without user-impact or action semantics.
  3. The four golden signals are a starting point for user-facing services, not a complete domain-specific monitoring specification.
  4. Monitoring and alerting do not replace durable business evidence, incident response or root-cause remediation.
