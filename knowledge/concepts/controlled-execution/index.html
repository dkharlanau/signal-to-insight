CONCEPT · agent architecture · explained

Controlled execution

A production work substrate that makes consequential execution authorized, observable, interruptible, reconstructable and testable.

agentsexecutiongovernance

EVIDENCE

Why this concept exists here.

PUBLISHED EXPLAINER

Why enterprise AI agents fail after the POC

A production agent needs a controlled execution environment around the model, not just a model connected to tools.

Source: Why Your Enterprise Tech Stack Isn't Ready for AI Agents - And What to Build Instead ↗

RELATIONS

What it depends on and connects to.

RELATED TO

Durable execution

Durability addresses continuity of work; controlled execution additionally requires authority, evidence, intervention and other governance controls.

DEPENDS ON

Execution history

Consequential work cannot be reconstructed or evaluated later without a durable history of execution.

DEPENDS ON

Human-agent task equivalence

Production work needs a stable task model when execution moves between an agent and a human reviewer.

DEPENDS ON

Observability

Operators need structured telemetry to inspect execution health even when business evidence lives in a separate durable record.

DEPENDS ON

Policy as code

Explicit policy decisions make authorization boundaries reviewable and testable instead of implicit in application code.

DEPENDS ON

Protected data separation

Governed orchestration should not require broad access to protected data simply to observe or control work.

BOUNDARIES

Where supporting models are incomplete.

  • An append-only history still needs identity evidence, policy and component versions, retention, tamper protection and meaningful event semantics.Evidence →
  • Separating data and orchestration reduces exposure but does not automatically eliminate prompt injection or data exfiltration.Evidence →
  • Human handoff still requires ownership, locking, idempotency, approval authority, timeout and recovery rules.Evidence →
  • Replay is only useful when reconstructed context and evaluation criteria are trustworthy.Evidence →
