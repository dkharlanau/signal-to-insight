The product loop

Not “save and summarize.” Compile trusted knowledge.

The durable asset is not a page. It is a cumulative model with provenance, stable concepts, explicit evidence boundaries and review state.

01Source

Canonical URL, creator, dates and provenance.

02Whole-source map

Problem, thesis, mechanisms, evidence and limitations before compression.

03Knowledge Delta

New, reinforced, refined or contradicted knowledge — with noise suppressed.

04Source Decision

Consume, skim, explainer-is-enough or skip-for-now.

05Explainer

Visual, coherent and generated from structured reviewed knowledge.

06Reconstruct

Test whether the mental model survives after reading.

Why this is different

Four outputs a normal summary does not give you.

Delta

What changed?

Every review-ready source is compared against existing concept memory. Attractive but irrelevant matches are recorded as noise instead of becoming fake graph edges.

Evidence

Why believe it?

Important claims carry source, verification or prior-insight evidence. Project interpretation is labeled separately from source-authored claims.

Decision

Do I need the original?

Source Decision is made only after whole-source mapping. `skim_selected_parts` locators must be backed by the same claim-evidence trace.

Retention

Can I reconstruct it later?

Each insight has authored reconstruction and transfer prompts. Real delayed scores remain a human benchmark rather than a generated confidence number.

Public proof

One intentionally published case. Nineteen remain review-only.

Publication is a trust boundary, not a content-volume target. The 20-source cohort was used to expose failures in retrieval, evidence linking, public projection and parallel agent execution before expanding the platform.
