## [0.1.0] - 2026-09-01

### Added

- Deterministic source intake, whole-source research bundles, claim evidence, prerequisites, and review-state validation.
- Cumulative knowledge graph, Knowledge Delta, Source Decision, learning prompts, and visual explainer generation.
- Explicit human publication/retraction workflows and private-overlay boundaries.
- Frozen 20-source structural validation cohort and retrieval regression benchmark.
- Public library, concept graph, discovery bundle, sitemap, and one reviewed proof walkthrough.
- Digest-protected public research-evidence handoff for bounded external consumption.

### Boundaries

- This release packages source and public artifacts; it does not publish review-only insights or private research inputs.
- One insight is published and nineteen remain in review by design. No external delayed-recall result is claimed.
- External research tooling is required for source capture/verification; the repository is not a universal browser, transcription, PDF, or LLM runtime.
