Portable contract · version 1.0.0

Research evidence, without operational authority.

Export one reviewed, published insight as a compact JSON packet for a separate human review or control-design workflow. The packet preserves provenance and claim-level support without carrying transcripts, private overlays or review-only material.

Fail-closed boundary Trust level is external_research_context. Human review is required. The packet cannot authorize execution, prove that a production incident occurred or change policy automatically.

Export and validate

python sti.py handoff export \
  enterprise-agents-production-substrate \
  --output /tmp/enterprise-agent-evidence.json

python sti.py handoff validate \
  /tmp/enterprise-agent-evidence.json

Only status=published insights can cross this boundary. Validation checks the known schema and version, required operational restrictions, absence of raw-source fields and the SHA-256 digest over the canonical payload.

The digest detects mutation. It is not a digital signature and does not establish who transported the file.

What crosses the boundary

Included

  • Public insight identity and link
  • Source title and canonical URL
  • Claim origin and support status
  • Claim-level evidence links
  • Explicit operational restrictions

Excluded

  • Transcripts and copied article text
  • PDF or raw-source dumps
  • Private and local overlays
  • Draft or review-only insights
  • Capabilities, approvals or execution rights

Use with SAP Agentic Operations

sao research validate /tmp/enterprise-agent-evidence.json
sao research review /tmp/enterprise-agent-evidence.json \
  --output /tmp/enterprise-agent-review.md

SAP Agentic Operations consumes the same v1 contract as external research context. Its review card preserves claim origin and source links, repeats the non-operational boundary, and does not merge research claims into an Evidence Pack.

Contract and reproducibility

  • Machine-readable JSON Schema
  • Deterministic reference packet
  • Markdown source documentation
  • Consumer documentation
python sti.py handoff export \
  enterprise-agents-production-substrate \
  --output examples/research-evidence-handoff/enterprise-agents-production-substrate.json \
  --check

If the published insight, source provenance or claim evidence changes, regenerate the packet intentionally and review the diff before publication. Consumers reject an unknown schema or major version.
