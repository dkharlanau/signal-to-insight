# Source & provenance policy

Signal to Insight is a commentary and explanation project, not a mirror of third-party material.

## Store

- source title and canonical link;
- author / speaker / publisher metadata when useful;
- original notes and derived insights;
- independently created diagrams and applications;
- short quotations only when they materially improve analysis;
- links to supporting or contradictory evidence.

## Do not store

- full third-party transcripts;
- copied articles or documentation;
- downloadable copies of source media;
- large excerpts that substitute for the original source.

## Review questions

Before publishing an explainer:

1. Is the source clearly attributed?
2. Is the central insight written in our own analytical framing?
3. Did we distinguish source claims from our interpretation?
4. Did we identify important limitations or counterexamples?
5. Is there a concrete application rather than only a summary?
6. Can a reader reach the original source directly?

The goal is transformation: **source → model → application**, not source → shorter source.
